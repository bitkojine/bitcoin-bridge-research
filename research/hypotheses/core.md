# Core hypothesis

The durable businesses will make Bitcoin legible and usable to institutions without weakening the properties that make Bitcoin valuable.

## Falsification questions

- Do institutions ultimately prefer synthetic exposure that does not settle meaningful assets on-chain?
- Does custody consolidate into existing financial institutions rather than specialist bridge companies?
- Can standards commoditize the bridge layers and remove durable pricing power?
- Do legal and regulatory differences prevent a coherent global market?
